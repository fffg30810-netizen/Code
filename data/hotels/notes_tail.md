## What could not be collected, and why

- **Hostel private rooms in the search ranking**: Booking lists one unit per hostel (the dorm bed), so hostel private rooms could only be checked on property pages for a few dates (section above); they are not part of the per-date top-8. Within 1.5 km there are only two hostels anyway (BEDS&ROOMS TIBURTINA Beds at 0.2 km, whose private rooms are the separate Booking listing "BEDS&ROOMS TIBURTINA- Rooms" that *is* in the ranking; Roma Scout Center at 0.7 km, whose private double is far more expensive than the guesthouses).
- **Second-site cross-check**: Trivago (HTTP 403), Kayak (redirects to landing page), Google Hotels (ignores dates/currency in the URL; date picker not drivable headlessly), Bing (bot-poisoned results), DuckDuckGo (connection reset by the proxy) were all unusable; Agoda worked but only exposes its property-level "from" price per night without the room grid. So the cross-check is per night, before taxes, not a like-for-like stay total.
- **Direct (official website) prices**: 21 Aprile Accommodation and BEDS&ROOMS TIBURTINA have no findable official website; Gmm's site takes bookings by phone/WhatsApp only (no prices); B&B Anturium's engine only shows an undated base rate. Only GH Hotel San Giusto has a real online engine, and its direct price was higher than Booking on all three dates checked.
- **Rooms for 2 adults**: checked only for the 10 cheapest combos, as requested (`two_adults_check.csv`).
- **Ranking exactness beyond the 8th place**: the price-band paging stops as soon as the 8 cheapest within 1.5 km are guaranteed; the 9th+ candidates in the parsed JSON are complete only up to the last price band fetched.
- **Distances** are Booking's straight-line distance to "Stazione Ferroviaria di Roma Tiburtina"; walking distance is longer (e.g. B&B SPQR, 0.35 km straight-line, is about 10 minutes on foot across the Circonvallazione Nomentana).
- **Prices move**: everything here is a snapshot of 2026-09-07 (12:44–13:53 UTC for the search passes, 16:1x–16:4x UTC for the cross-checks). Booking's totals are shown with Booking's "paga online" discounts where applied ("Prezzo iniziale/attuale"); `before_discount_eur` in the parsed JSON keeps the undiscounted number.
